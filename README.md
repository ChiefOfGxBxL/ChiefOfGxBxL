### 📖 Etymology shenanigans
I have a newly-found appreciation for word origins. Below are some I find interesting.

*Did you know...*  
  * the word *sabotage* comes from the French *sabot*, a type of shoe; to sabotage would mean to throw your shoe(s) at something as to break it
  * the word *curfew* comes from the French *couvre feu*, meaning "cover fire"; a mandate was implemented for townspeople to put out their fires by a certain time at night for safety
  * the word *vinegar* comes from the French *vin aigre*, meaning sour wine
  * the word *library* comes from the Latin prefix *libr-*, referring to the inner bark of trees, upon which things were written
  * the word *copper* is named after Cyprus, from which Romans mined their copper
  * the word *platinum* comes from Spanish *platina*, a diminuative of *plata* meaning silver or plate
  * the word *iridium* comes from Latin *iris/irid*, meaning rainbow
